Frequently asked questions

Clear answers on engineering work and collaboration.

What types of software projects do you specialize in?

I build full-stack web applications, RESTful APIs, database architectures, and internal productivity systems. My focus is on creating reliable, maintainable codebases for operational workflows.

Are you available for engineering roles or contracts?

Yes, I am actively open to software engineering internships, full-time junior engineering positions, and contract development roles worldwide.

What is your approach to application security?

Security is built-in from day one. I enforce strict input validation, safe authentication mechanisms, role-based access control, and follow Cisco cybersecurity guidelines in every project.

How do you handle team communication and updates?

I communicate technical decisions clearly, document API contracts thoroughly, and provide regular progress updates through Git commits and project dashboards.

Can you work across both frontend and backend stacks?

Yes, I work fluently across React/Next.js on the frontend and Node.js/Express with MongoDB or PostgreSQL on the backend.

How quickly can we get started on a project?

I can review project requirements and provide a technical breakdown within 24 hours. Get in touch directly via LinkedIn or email.
